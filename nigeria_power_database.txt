--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1 — WHAT THIS DATABASE IS AND ISN'T
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- This is NOT a live outage log. No public source publishes per-city or
  per-street real-time outage data for Nigeria — not PHCN/DisCos, not social
  media aggregators. That dataset doesn't exist yet; the app itself is meant
  to start building it from user reports.
- What IS real and verified (as of Sept 2026): DisCo/state coverage, the NERC
  Band system, national generation figures, grid collapse frequency, and
  known vandalism/infrastructure trouble spots. Sources listed in Section 6.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2 — NERC BAND SYSTEM (Service-Based Tariff, since April 2024)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Band A : 20-24 hours/day guaranteed supply — highest tariff (~N209-225+/kWh)
Band B : 16-19 hours/day
Band C : 12-15 hours/day
Band D : 8-11 hours/day
Band E : 4-7 hours/day — lowest tariff

Notes:
- Bands are assigned per FEEDER (a transformer's service line), not per city
  or state. Two streets in the same neighborhood can be on different bands.
- Only ~15% of Nigeria's ~12 million registered electricity customers are on
  Band A.
- Compliance is inconsistent: NERC has repeatedly downgraded/upgraded feeders
  month to month (see IBEDC example below) after DisCos failed to sustain
  promised hours. Multiple 2025-2026 investigative reports found "Band A"
  feeders in Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt and Calabar delivering below the
  promised 20 hours.
- DisCos are technically required to publish a rolling 7-day average supply
  figure for Band A feeders on their own websites by 9:00am daily — this is
  a real, scrapeable data source for a future live version of this app.

SECTION 5 — NATIONAL GENERATION & GRID FACTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Installed generation capacity: ~13,600 MW across 28 grid-connected plants (2025)
- Actual AVAILABLE generation: typically ~5,000-5,400 MW (far below installed
  capacity); can crash to a few hundred MW during a collapse
- Generation mix: ~80% gas-fired thermal, ~20% hydropower (Kainji, Jebba, Shiroro)
- National grid collapses: 220+ recorded partial/total collapses since 2010;
  roughly one per month in recent years (12 in 2024; several more through
  2025-2026)
- A single grid collapse can black out multiple states simultaneously
  (documented joint outages across Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Kano, Enugu,
  Kaduna)
- GenCo debt: grew to an estimated N6.6 trillion (Jan 2026), N6.8 trillion
  (Feb 2026), projected N8.8 trillion by Dec 2026 if unresolved. Of every
  N280bn in monthly GenCo invoices, only ~35% is typically paid.

SECTION 6 — VANDALISM / INFRASTRUCTURE INCIDENT LOG (illustrative, not exhaustive)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- TCN logged 131 transmission-infrastructure vandalism cases Jan-Nov 2025
- 178 transmission towers affected in H1 2025 alone (42 separate incidents)
- 2024: 86 towers vandalized nationwide, 26 completely destroyed
- Jan 2025: vandals damaged towers 105-107 on the Katsina-Gazoua 132/33kV line
- 2024: Ahoada-Yenagoa transmission corridor destroyed -> ~4-month blackout
  across Bayelsa State
- Highest-reported vandalism/cable-theft states: Abia, Akwa Ibom, Ekiti, Rivers
- Copper cable and transformer-part theft is often linked to resale as scrap
  or repurposing for informal solar setups

SECTION 7 — SUGGESTED LIVE-DATA ROADMAP (for turning this into a real product)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Backend + real database (Postgres/Mongo) to persist signups, survey
   answers, and outage reports permanently (the current index.html keeps
   everything in browser memory only — it resets on refresh).
2. Real authentication (hashed passwords, sessions/JWT) — the current
   prototype does NOT securely store passwords; do not reuse real passwords
   with it.
3. Geolocation + clustering logic: group reports within a radius/time window
   into a single "confirmed cluster" event (this is mocked with random
   numbers in the current build).
4. A scheduled job (e.g. hourly cron) that:
     - polls DisCo websites for their published Band A 7-day rolling average
     - runs a news-API or RSS search for terms like "transformer", "NEPA",
       "PHCN", "gas crisis", "TCN vandalism", per state
     - posts matching items into the Infrastructure Intel feed, tagged by
       state/LGA
   This is the only realistic way to do the "scan the internet automatically"
   feature — a static HTML file cannot do this on its own; it needs a server
   that runs continuously.
5. Push/SMS/WhatsApp alerts to users in an affected area when a new cluster
   or news item is posted for their state/city.
6. A real map (e.g. Leaflet/Mapbox with Nigeria GeoJSON boundaries) in place
   of the current state-tile grid, once you have real lat/lon-tagged reports.
